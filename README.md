### 🥗 [Kcalm](https://github.com/Tseende/Kcalm)

A Telegram food diary that never shames you. Snap a plate, say it out loud or type it, and the bot works out
calories and macros. A Mini App inside Telegram shows daily stats, weight trends and tips built from your own data.
The LLM only recognizes the food; Python does all the math, so the numbers stay reproducible and testable.

### 📷 [CamDrop](https://apps.apple.com/by/app/camdrop-raw-photo-transfer/id6817102210)

An iPhone and iPad app that imports photos, full RAW (ARW) and video from Sony cameras over Wi-Fi.
No Sony account and no subscription. Cameras from 2020 on connect over PTP/IP with full RAW,
older ones over DLNA. Collects no user data. Available on the App Store.
